# Roadmap

## Vision

AskPlevy exists to explore how artificial intelligence is transforming professional expertise, trust, and discoverability.

The objective is to build a knowledge platform that helps professionals structure expertise so it can be understood, surfaced, and applied effectively by both people and AI systems.

Rather than simply writing about the future of AI, AskPlevy is built to experiment with it.

---

## Current Stage

Current development focuses on AI-native publishing, structured knowledge systems, and experimentation with AI Search and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

Current priorities include:

* Building structured knowledge resources
* Expanding AI Search experimentation
* Implementing structured data
* Improving information architecture
* Developing AI-friendly content workflows
* Measuring AI discoverability

## Long-Term Goals

* Become a leading resource for AI-first professional strategy
* Publish original research on AI discoverability
* Build tools that help professionals adapt to AI Search
* Continue experimenting with emerging AI interfaces
* Connect applied AI research with products such as Dottie Deeds and Wolf Allies

---

## Guiding Principle

Professional expertise is becoming increasingly discoverable through artificial intelligence rather than traditional search.

The goal of AskPlevy is to understand that transformation through experimentation, thoughtful product design, and practical implementation—not theory alone.
