About Wolf Allies

Helping families and fiduciaries understand California real estate.

As a licensed California Real Estate Broker, William Plevy created Wolf Allies (wolfallies.com), a platform that helps families with inherited and probate property connect with experienced local real estate agents.

For many families, dealing with California real estate in the aftermath of a loved one's death is their first encounter with probate law, trust administration, property tax reassessment, and fiduciary obligations. The learning curve is steep, and the stakes, both financial and emotional, are high. Wolf Allies is designed to help families understand the landscape and connect with the right professionals.

The platform covers inherited real estate, trust-owned property, probate sales, trustee responsibilities, Proposition 19 and property tax issues, family property transfers, and related topics. Content is written in plain language for property owners, beneficiaries, trustees, executors, and family members, not for lawyers.

Wolf Allies covers:

  • Inherited California real estate
  • Trust-owned property and trustee duties
  • Probate real estate in California
  • Proposition 19 and property tax reassessment
  • Family property transfers
  • Trustee and executor responsibilities
  • Sibling co-ownership issues
  • Insurance, mortgage, and maintenance during administration
  • Sale vs. retention of inherited property
  • Step-up in basis and income tax considerations

Wolf Allies is a separate resource and does not create an attorney-client or brokerage relationship. For professional inquiries, contact William through Ask Plevy.
