Attorney Profile

William Plevy

William Plevy is an attorney whose practice focuses on business law, real estate law, and advanced planning. He works with business owners, investors, trustees, and families on matters involving ownership structures, transactions, succession planning, and real estate.

William is licensed to practice law in California, New York, and Washington, D.C. He is also a California Real Estate Broker, providing a practical perspective on the legal and business issues that arise in property ownership and real estate transactions.
