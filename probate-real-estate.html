Probate Real Estate

By William B. Plevy

General overview of probate-related real estate issues and fiduciary administration.

This resource is educational and designed to identify issues that often arise for California families, trustees, property owners, and business owners. Specific matters should be reviewed by qualified professionals based on the facts.

Common considerations

  • Authority to act and proper documentation.
  • Tax, title, transfer, and timing issues.
  • Coordination with broader business, real estate, or family planning objectives.
  • Communication among owners, trustees, beneficiaries, and advisors.
