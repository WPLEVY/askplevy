# AskPlevy

**Exploring How Artificial Intelligence Is Reshaping Professional Expertise**

AskPlevy is a living research project exploring how artificial intelligence is transforming the way professionals build trust, share knowledge, and become discoverable.

The project began with a simple question.

**What happens when people stop searching for professionals and start asking AI instead?**

Rather than simply writing about that future, I decided to build, experiment, and document what I learn in public.

AskPlevy serves as both a professional platform and an applied research laboratory focused on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI-native publishing, structured knowledge, and the future of professional services.

---

# Why I Built AskPlevy

For decades, professionals built websites primarily for search engines.

Today, people increasingly ask AI systems questions instead of browsing pages of search results.

That changes everything.

Professional authority is no longer determined solely by backlinks and keyword rankings.

It is increasingly influenced by how well expertise is structured, understood, and synthesized by artificial intelligence.

I built AskPlevy to better understand that transition through experimentation rather than speculation.

Every article, workflow, and architectural decision is an opportunity to test how professionals can build trust in an AI-first world.

---

# The Problem

Artificial intelligence is changing how knowledge is discovered.

Traditional websites were designed for search engines.

The next generation of professional platforms must also communicate effectively with AI systems.

That requires new approaches to:

* Structured information
* Knowledge organization
* Authority building
* Content architecture
* Machine-readable context
* Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
* AI Search

Most professionals continue optimizing for yesterday's internet.

AskPlevy explores what comes next.

---

AskPlevy combines thoughtful writing, structured publishing, and applied AI experimentation to explore how professionals can remain discoverable and valuable as AI becomes the primary interface between people and information.

# My Philosophy

I don't start with AI.

I start with broken workflows.

After spending more than fifteen years solving complex legal and business problems, I realized that the greatest opportunities for artificial intelligence aren't found by asking, "Where can we use AI?"

They're found by asking, "Where is valuable human time being wasted?"

My approach is simple.

Understand the domain.

Map the workflow.

Identify the friction.

Then build software that removes it.

I don't believe AI should replace professionals.

I believe it should remove repetitive work so professionals can spend more time exercising judgment, solving difficult problems, and building meaningful relationships.

Every product I build reflects that philosophy.
